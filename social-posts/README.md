## Two things to decide before posting the concepts

**The concept posts name real businesses you pitched and didn't win.** Your
website handles this properly — a Concept badge and an explicit "not a
commissioned project" line. On social the label is smaller and posts get shared
out of context, so each concept caption above opens by saying it plainly. Keep
that first line. If you'd rather not show their branding at all, posting only
the five client cards is a perfectly strong launch.

**The JPM demo displays "5.0 Google rating" and "40+ Google reviews".** Those
are placeholder numbers in a mockup for a business that didn't commission it.
It's visible in the card. Worth regenerating that demo with the stats removed
before posting, or dropping post 9 and running with eight.

They are all concepts, so **do not post them in a block.** Twelve unpaid

of true. Thread them between the client work and the review cards, and open
